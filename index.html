Why the days sit in this order

Thursday

You land at 15:20 after nearly ten hours in the air. Anything formal before seven would be unkind, so the evening is warm, close and easy.

Friday

The first fully rested day carries the anniversary, and it ends without leaving the building. Nothing about the celebration depends on traffic.

Saturday

One of only four days a week St George's Chapel opens to visitors, so Windsor belongs here. The evening then belongs to the river.

Sunday

Nothing significant opens in London before half past eleven on a Sunday, which is exactly why the Tower takes the morning and the arcade takes the afternoon.
